Brooklyn, 1964 to today

One guitarist. Sixty years of New York hard rock.

Billi Pepa started The Starfires with his brother in Brooklyn in 1964. This archive follows the music from there — The Front End on Smash Records, the trio Pepa.Beads.Kydd, Jimmibeetles Rock Theatre at CBGB, and the Best of The Wildstarfires albums you can hear today.

      About this archive

      The band's home base is thewildstarfires.com, built and looked after by Andrew Persaud, who also designed the Best Of albums. Every lyric lives there, so this archive links to it instead of copying it. This site gathers the stories and photos in one searchable place and points back to the official pages.

      Everything here comes from Billi Pepa's own site, his album covers and liner notes, and the recordings on Bandcamp and Spotify. Where a fact comes from somewhere else — like The Front End's singles or a collector's page about the Jimmibeetles legend — the entry says so. The photos are real. The video series Lamar is making is AI-made, and it is always labeled that way.

      Who Jimmibeetles really was is part of the story, and it stays a mystery here on purpose.
